About

From product imports to integration architecture.

How it started

I graduated from INSEA Rabat in 2015 as a State Engineer in Computer Science & BI, and started with an internship at Brake.fr — my first contact with SAP Commerce Cloud, then called Hybris.

What began with product import flows and CMS customisations turned into a lasting interest in how enterprise systems talk to each other. That curiosity is what moved me from SAP Commerce into API integration, and eventually to MuleSoft Anypoint.

What I work on now

I'm at Roche Bobois through SQLI, based in Nantes: orchestration patterns, workflow optimisation and error handling that keep order, CRM and ERP flows reliable. SAP Commerce taught me the business logic of commerce; MuleSoft is where I apply it across systems.

AI is part of how I deliver, not a talking point. I use GitHub Copilot day to day, a CLI of my own that drafts commit messages and pull requests from diffs with local and cloud LLMs, and Claude and OpenAI Codex CLI for review and generation.

What I enjoy most is mentoring junior developers and weighing in on architecture decisions with long-term impact. The best solutions come from understanding both the implementation and the business value it delivers.
