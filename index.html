01 — What I do

The layer in the middle.

The storefront and the back office each do their job. My work is the part that makes them talk: reusable APIs, data mapping and error handling that hold up in production.

Commerce

SAP Commerce Cloud

  • CCv2 · OCC APIs
  • Spartacus storefronts
  • B2B and B2C catalogues
⇄

Integration layer

MuleSoft Anypoint

  • Experience APIs
  • Process APIs · orchestration
  • System APIs · DataWeave mapping
⇄

Back office

Salesforce · ERP

  • CRM data flows
  • Pricing, inventory, orders
  • PIM · OMS · payments

API-led connectivity keeps the System, Process and Experience layers separate, so one system can change without breaking the others.
