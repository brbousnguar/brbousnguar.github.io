As technical lead, I owned the architecture and the migration to SAP Commerce Cloud (CCv2): one platform for every country, fed with real-time ERP data.

## What I built

- The architecture and migration from SAP Hybris 6.7 to Commerce Cloud CCv2.
- SAP ERP integrations for pricing, inventory and orders, plus third-party logistics and payment providers.
- Custom OCC APIs for headless B2B workflows.
- Performance work and technical SEO for organic traffic.

I also mentored the junior developers on SAP Commerce, Spring and integration patterns.

## Where it landed

One B2B platform serving 15+ countries, with real-time ERP data and headless commerce.
